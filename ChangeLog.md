# CHANGELOG MODULE INSTALLMENTS FOR [DOLIBARR ERP CRM](https://www.dolibarr.org)

## 1.2.0

- **Cash forecast of a bank account** (Banks > account > Upcoming entries): one row per installment left to pay, at its date and with its rank (`FA2611-0001 (2/3)`), instead of the whole rest of the invoice on one date; the future balance stays the one of Dolibarr (see the README). New hook context `banktreso`, so **after an update from 1.1.0, disable and enable the module again** (seen on Dolibarr 24.0.2 with the files of the zip 1.2.0 copied over 1.1.0: nothing changes before; the jobs with their state, the rights and the schedules are kept after). The disabling done with the files of 1.2.0 in place keeps the constants `INSTALLMENTS_START_DATE` and `INSTALLMENTS_DEBIT_START_DATE` (dates of the first runs of the jobs; 1.1.0 deleted the first one at each disabling, so the job skipped the installments due meanwhile).
- FIX: the list and the widget count as paid what the core counts: the discounts used as payments coming from a standard invoice (excess received) and from a situation invoice, with the credit notes and the deposits (an installment paid by an excess received was shown to pay).
- FIX (review of 13/11/2026, from 1.1.0): never invoiced twice (the invoices linked to the document or given to its installments, abandoned ones left out, credit notes deducted; an invoice of another kind given to an installment: the last one in proportion), document classified billed once all its installments are invoiced; import: only an invoice linked to the document is attached, another one suggested in the note, documents of another entity left out, installments following the mode of their document; an installment to check never invoiced by hand or in mass, kept by a new calculation, "Invoice again" once checked; a schedule made on a draft put to the scale of the invoice at its validation; mass payment: never more than the rest of the invoice, an invoice with a direct debit in progress skipped, reference of the transfer; direct debits: never a disputed invoice, nothing before the first run (`INSTALLMENTS_DEBIT_START_DATE`); mode "validated and sent": a template required (never "the first template found"), an invoice not sent puts its installment to check until it is sent from its card; "Already invoiced", "Abandon" and "Not this invoice" need the right to create invoices and write an event, an invoice smaller than the installment refused; the list shows only the installments of the documents the user may read; invoices of installments with the designations of the document, in its currency and at its rate; local taxes no longer counted twice; PDF: payment terms "As per the payment schedule", all the installments (two columns beyond 12), amounts in the currency of the document, the document itself never changed (a copy prints the note); a date typed before the document refused; warning at the activation about the reminder jobs of Dolibarr; the dates of the first runs of the jobs kept by an update (disable then enable): a module enabled again after a long stop invoices, and asks the direct debits of, the installments due meanwhile. Second review: an installment to check whose invoice is validated by hand is no longer to check, "Checked" for an invoiced installment to check; direct debits never more than the rest of the invoice; a document billed when its last installment is linked or abandoned; the reasons of a check never printed on the invoice; one cent of rounding per installment allowed by the check of double invoicing (local taxes); a schedule of 1.1.0 in mode "validated and sent" with the template "Default": a warning on its tab; a direct debit job of 1.1.0 that already asked keeps asking the installments due before the update.

## 1.1.0

- **Technical name `installments`** (folder, classes, tables `llx_installments_*`, constants `INSTALLMENTS_*`): the paid module Payment Schedule of ATM uses the names of 1.0.0. Disable and remove 1.0.0 first; its schedules are not kept (it was published one day before).
- Templates with free installments (`30:0;30:30;40:60:eom`) and linked to a payment term: an invoice, a proposal or an order validated with this payment term gets the schedule.
- Schedule in the PDF of the proposals and orders (in their public note while the PDF is made).
- Import from Echeancier: the invoices made by hand are found for the installments (same amount within 45 days, linked to the document first); the others come "To check", past or future, never invoiced by the job.
- A refused, draft or cancelled proposal or order is never invoiced; an invoice is never emailed without its PDF; local taxes kept in the invoices of the installments; an invoice linked to one installment only.
- List and widget: expected payments of proposals and orders on their tab only, installments of an abandoned invoice abandoned.
- Triggers return their errors (the core cancels its action); payment modes added by the user shown with their label.
- Direct debit of one installment: the other installments keep the mode they had from the invoice (the core sets the mode of the invoice to direct debit). A schedule saved again keeps its installments to check and abandoned; an abandoned installment is never invoiced with the last one.
- A schedule of an order or proposal not changed with its document is not copied to its invoice, and a template that can not apply is said: the validation always goes on.
- Checked with phpcs, phpstan, phan and the coding tests of Dolibarr (`build/check.sh`, in the CI too); tests on MySQL and PostgreSQL, Dolibarr 24.0 and develop.

- What is paid given to the installments by date; due date of the invoice on the next unpaid installment, so the core shows the right delay.

- Schedule under the totals of the PDF of the invoice, key `__PAYMENTSCHEDULE__`, list of the installments with mass payments, tab of the customer, widget.
